# Candidate validation and test datasets

Assuming the training corpus consists of ISSP (2015 to 2024), WVS Wave 7, Twin-2K-500, and the Twin-2K-500 Mega-Study, the datasets below provide intact U.S. respondent microdata from independent survey organizations with zero overlap in respondents or questionnaires. Whenever available, downloading the Stata (.dta) release alongside the questionnaire or codebook PDF simplifies downstream parsing because Stata files preserve variable labels and response option text.

## ANES 2024 time series study

Run by Stanford University and the University of Michigan, the ANES 2024 Time Series Study is a nationally representative probability sample of roughly 5,500 U.S. adults covering more than 300 attitude items across gender, race, immigration, abortion, climate, economic policy, institutional trust, media, and social norms. It matches all 12 canonical persona attributes in our schema, including political party, political ideology, and religion. Free public microdata and documentation are available after signing into a free account on the [ANES 2024 Time Series Study page](https://electionstudies.org/data-center/2024-time-series-study/) or through the main [ANES Data Center](https://electionstudies.org/data-center/).

## Cooperative Election Study 2024 common content

Conducted by Harvard University, Tufts University, and YouGov under a CC0 license, the 2024 CES Common Content (V9) covers 60,000 American adults across roughly 80 to 100 opinion, social-issue, economic, healthcare, climate, and institutional attitude items. It includes all 12 canonical persona attributes and provides unusually high statistical power for intersectional multi-trait subgroups. No login is required to access the [Harvard Dataverse dataset page](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/X11EP6) or its direct files, which include the [Stata dataset (993 MB)](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/12050327), the [CSV dataset (184 MB)](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/12050325), the [CES 2024 Guide PDF](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/12050326), the [pre-election questionnaire](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/11032062), and the [post-election questionnaire](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/api/access/datafile/11049825).

## Federal Reserve survey of household economics and decisionmaking

Fielded by the Federal Reserve Board via Ipsos KnowledgePanel with roughly 11,500 U.S. adult respondents per wave, SHED measures consumer finance, spending, inflation expectations, credit and Buy-Now-Pay-Later usage, housing, financial well-being, banking, higher-education returns, and remote or gig work. It covers 9 of the 12 canonical persona attributes, omitting political party, political ideology, and religion. All releases are publicly hosted without login on the [Federal Reserve SHED data page](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed_data.htm). For the newest 2025 release, direct downloads are available for the [SHED 2025 Stata ZIP](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/files/SHED_public_use_data_2025_%28STATA%29.zip), the [SHED 2025 CSV ZIP](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/files/SHED_public_use_data_2025_%28CSV%29.zip), and the [SHED 2025 Codebook PDF](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/files/SHED_2025codebook.pdf). For the 2024 release, direct downloads are available for the [SHED 2024 Stata ZIP](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/files/SHED_public_use_data_2024_%28STATA%29.zip), the [SHED 2024 CSV ZIP](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/files/SHED_public_use_data_2024_%28CSV%29.zip), and the [SHED 2024 Codebook PDF](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/files/SHED_2024codebook.pdf).

## NCI health information national trends survey 7

Administered by the National Cancer Institute in 2024 with 7,278 U.S. adult respondents, HINTS 7 covers consumer media and social media habits, trust in information sources, privacy and data sharing, digital health and wearable technology adoption, and lifestyle attitudes. Like SHED, it provides 9 demographic and socioeconomic persona attributes without political party, political ideology, or religion. Public files require no login on the [HINTS download page](https://hints.cancer.gov/data/download-data.aspx), with direct packages (including codebooks and supporting documents) for the [HINTS 7 Stata ZIP (20.9 MB)](https://hints.cancer.gov/dataset/HINTS7_STATA_20250731.zip), the [HINTS 7 SPSS ZIP (20.6 MB)](https://hints.cancer.gov/dataset/HINTS7_SPSS_20250731.zip), and the [HINTS 7 R ZIP (19.6 MB)](https://hints.cancer.gov/dataset/HINTS7_R_20250731.zip).

## Pew Research Center American Trends Panel

Pew Research Center's nationally representative American Trends Panel releases respondent-level microdata for individual waves of roughly 5,000 to 10,000 U.S. adults covering technology, AI, media, social trust, consumer economy, and public opinion, paired with core demographic and political attributes. Downloads require a free account through the [Pew registration page](https://www.pewresearch.org/profile/registration/). Waves can be browsed on the [American Trends Panel datasets index](https://www.pewresearch.org/american-trends-panel-datasets/) or the general [Pew datasets browser](https://www.pewresearch.org/datasets/), accompanied by the [ATP demographic profile codebook PDF](https://www.pewresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Codebook-and-instructions-for-working-with-ATP-data.pdf). Recent 2025 examples include [Wave 169 on climate and energy](https://www.pewresearch.org/dataset/american-trends-panel-wave-169/), [Wave 168 on online fraud and journalists](https://www.pewresearch.org/dataset/american-trends-panel-wave-168/), and [Wave 165 on media brands and social trust](https://www.pewresearch.org/dataset/american-trends-panel-wave-165/).
