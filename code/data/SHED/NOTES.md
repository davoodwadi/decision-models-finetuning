# Federal Reserve SHED 2025

## Dataset overview

The Federal Reserve Board Survey of Household Economics and Decisionmaking (SHED) 2025 fields a nationally representative Ipsos KnowledgePanel sample of 12,934 U.S. adults covering household financial well-being, inflation shopping responses, credit cards, Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL), banking, cryptocurrency, fraud, emergency savings, retirement planning, housing, medical and food affordability, and generative AI adoption at work. Raw data live in `SHED_public_use_data_2025_(STATA).zip` (`public2025.dta`, 815 columns) alongside the official 119-page `SHED_2025codebook.pdf`. Because SHED 2025 focuses squarely on U.S. consumer behavior and everyday household financial decisions, it serves as a dedicated consumer benchmark alongside NCI HINTS 7.

## Persona harmonization

The standardized persona loader in `code/data/persona_loaders.py` (`load_raw_shed2025_personas`) maps 19 canonical `persona_spec.yaml` attributes onto `CASEID` (`shedid`): `country` (`US`), `age` (`ppage`), `gender` (`ppgender`), `region` (`ppreg9`, mapped to the nine U.S. Census divisions), `state` (`ppstaten`, 51 lowercase USPS codes), `urban_rural` (`ppmsacat`), `education` (`ED0`, 99.7% non-imputed), `family_income` (`I40`, 98.7% non-imputed across 15 brackets), `race` (`ppracem`), `hispanic` (`pphispan`), `marital_status` (`ppmarit5`), `employment_status` (`ppemploy`), `household_size` (`pphhsize`), `children_in_household` (`ppkid017`), `residence_type` (`pphouse4`), `citizen` (`ppp20197`, 91.7%), `sexual_orientation` (`pppa_lgb`, 90.1%), `union_member` (`UNION100`, 52.5%), and `employer_sector` (`ppcm1301`, 61.5%). Every Ipsos KnowledgePanel profile column (`pp*`) and all mapped persona source columns (`ED0`, `I40`, `UNION100`) are strictly refused from survey question export. SHED 2025 does not field political party, political ideology, or religion, which closely parallels the socioeconomic and demographic persona footprint of NCI HINTS 7.

## Imputation masking and data cleaning

SHED 2025 includes 357 hot-deck imputation indicator columns (`*_iflag`). Both `load_raw_shed2025_personas` (for `ED0` and `I40`) and `code/data/shed2025_export.py` (for every exported survey question) validate that each `{v}_iflag` takes values strictly in `{0, 1}` and mask `{v}_iflag == 1` to missing so exported datasets contain only genuine respondent-reported answers. Negative codes across `public2025.dta` are strictly validated against `{-9, -2, -1}` (`-1` Refused, `-2` Don't know or Not asked, `-9` substantive not-applicable items such as `C3P`, `SL23`, and `BK55`) and masked to missing. Zero-count duplicate value-label artifacts in the Stata metadata (such as code 4 repeating `No` on `D22_a` through `D22_i`) are dropped during option extraction. The 0 to 10 financial risk tolerance scale (`FL0`) uses `data.option_labels.format_option_texts` to render explicit numbered anchors and intermediate points (`0. Not at all willing to take financial risks` through `10. Very willing to take financial risks`).

## Question catalog and audit workflow

`code/data/SHED/build_question_catalog.py` parses `public2025.dta` and extracts untruncated verbatim wording from `SHED_2025codebook.pdf` via `pdftotext -layout`, producing `question_audit.csv` with 307 discrete survey questions after excluding Ipsos profile variables, administrative and derived fields, six continuous dollar open-ended items (`CG2`, `GH14`, `R3`, `M4`, `BK49A`, `BK49B`), and 40 single-option `Not selected` checkbox columns. By default, 117 questions are included (88 `choice`, 29 `score`), covering all full-sample consumer and financial batteries plus the two random 50% split ballots (`X12_a` through `X12_g` and `EF7` / `EF7A`). For 85 matrix items whose Stata codebook header places the sub-item before a dash and stem, `QuestionText` reorders the text into natural stem-then-sub-item reading order while retaining the verbatim codebook header in `codebook_text` and surfacing the diff in the Audit UI Source tab.

## Notable excluded items for review

By default, 190 items are excluded, primarily skip-gated subsamples (`N < 10,000`), living-arrangement roster items overlapping persona attributes (`L0_*`, `L0B_*`, `L0C`, `L0E`), student and retirement status items (`D1G`, `D1I`), the randomized pay-cut experiment (`D36B`, which injects `DOV_D36_AMOUNT`), `GH14A`, select-all follow-ups, and other-specify checkboxes. Several large consumer and workplace subsamples are excluded under the default subsample gate and warrant review in the Audit UI if conditional populations are desired, including homeowners insurance shopping and attitudes (`GH12`, `GH15`, `GH16_a` through `GH16_e`, `N` around 7,900 to 8,460), generative AI usage and attitudes at work (`D50`, `D52_a` through `D52_f`, `N` around 7,460), credit discouragement (`A0B`, `N = 8,611`), retirement plan confidence (`K0`, `N = 6,955`), and work-status overlap items (`D1A`, `D1B`, `D1E`) that are currently included and can be excluded if judged redundant with `employment_status`.

## Export artifacts

Running `PYTHONPATH=code .venv-cu13/bin/python code/data/shed2025_export.py` or clicking the Audit UI rebuild button atomically writes `shed2025_long.parquet` and `questions_verified.csv`. With the initial default catalog, `shed2025_long.parquet` contains 1,439,593 rows across 12,934 respondents, 117 questions (88 `choice`, 29 `score`), and 19 standardized persona columns (12,715 unique personas).
