# Data preprocessing plan for Grummon et al. (2023)

This document specifies the step-by-step preprocessing pipeline to convert the replication archive of Grummon et al. (2023, JAMA Network Open, 6(9), e2333515, `doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.33515`) in [ResearchBox 1895](./ResearchBox%201895) into a verified Kev training dataset (`grummon2023_US_long.parquet`) while keeping the Grummon et al. (2025) benchmark in [grummon2025_US_long.parquet](../grummon2025/grummon2025_US_long.parquet) strictly isolated as our out-of-study test set.

## Step 1. Raw ingestion, header normalization, and cohort filtering

Preprocessing starts from the raw Qualtrics export [VLO-Survey-2022-3+FINAL_December+5,+2022_09.36-NoIDs.csv](./ResearchBox%201895/Data/VLO-Survey-2022-3+FINAL_December+5,+2022_09.36-NoIDs.csv) ($10,055$ rows) alongside the product-level tables [BeverageCharacteristics.dta](./ResearchBox%201895/Data/BeverageCharacteristics.dta) ($16$ beverages) and [SnackCharacteristics.dta](./ResearchBox%201895/Data/SnackCharacteristics.dta) ($16$ snacks), replicating [00_AnalysisParentFile.do](./ResearchBox%201895/Code/00_AnalysisParentFile.do) and [01_DataPrep.do](./ResearchBox%201895/Code/01_DataPrep.do) with fail-fast assertions.

Before merging arm-specific columns, six raw Qualtrics header and coding quirks must be normalized explicitly. Two calorie-arm snack perception headers in the CSV contain an internal space (`"snack _healthyY_Cal"` and `"snack _healthyG_Cal"`, which Stata's `import delimited` stripped automatically) and must be renamed to `snack_healthyY_Cal` and `snack_healthyG_Cal`. Four calorie-arm stigma headers use uppercase `_CAL` (`stigma_personal_CAL`, `stigma_obesity_CAL`, `stigma_blame_CAL`, and `disgust_CAL`) and must be renamed to `_Cal`. All numeric and string occurrences of `-99` must be decoded to missing (`NaN`), and the Qualtrics response-code typo on `snack_healthyY_W` must be recoded from `10` to `5` (`Very healthy`), matching line 117 of [01_DataPrep.do](./ResearchBox%201895/Code/01_DataPrep.do).

Next, the five pre-registered CONSORT exclusions are applied sequentially, retaining respondents with `svy_status == "complete"`, `Q_TotalDuration >= 191.2` seconds (one-third of the $573.5$-second soft-launch median), `confirmkeepdata != 0` (did not request data removal at debrief), `consent != 0`, and `age >= 18`. The pipeline must assert that this filter yields exactly $N = 7,945$ analytic respondents distributed across `foplcond` codes `1` through `5` as $1,593$ in `Cal` (Calorie control), $1,587$ in `G` (Green only), $1,588$ in `TLL` (Single traffic light), $1,590$ in `PA` (Physical activity calorie equivalent), and $1,587$ in `W` (Nutrient warning), matching Figure 2 of the published article to the exact respondent. Each retained respondent receives a sequential `pid` ($1$ to $7,945$) and `CASEID = f"grummon2023_{pid}"`.

## Step 2. Verification against published tables and test-set isolation

Before constructing prompts, arm-merged columns (`<var>_merged`) are formed across `Cal`, `G`, `TLL`, `PA`, and `W` for all $32$ survey outcomes ($254,145$ non-missing participant-item observations in total) and cross-checked against the published article and Supplement 2. Specifically, merging `bev_choice` and `snack_choice` onto `BeverageCharacteristics.dta` and `SnackCharacteristics.dta` by `itemnumber_bev` and `itemnumber_snack` must reproduce the arm-level calorie means and average differential effects in Figure 3, eTable 5, and eTable 6 (e.g., beverage calorie reductions of $-34.2$, $-31.5$, $-39.0$, and $-28.2\text{ kcal}$ relative to `Cal`), while demographic summaries and secondary psychological means must match Table 1 and Table 2.

Test-set isolation against Grummon et al. (2025) is also verified at this stage. Our held-out evaluation file [grummon2025_US_long.parquet](../grummon2025/grummon2025_US_long.parquet) contains $21$ items across six 2025 labeling systems (`Positive`, `NutritionInfo`, `HighIn`, `Positive+NutritionInfo`, `Positive+HighIn`, and `Spectrum`), namely the six pairwise consumer-understanding tasks (`idbev`, `idbread`, `idcereal`, `idsoup`, `idsnack`, and `idmeal`) and $15$ post-shopping items. None of the pairwise product-comparison tasks, product pairs, or 2025 FDA/Guiding Stars label designs appear in Grummon et al. (2023). Several post-shopping stems are shared with Grummon et al. (2023) (`raffect_worried` through `raffect_sad`, `rtrust`, and `noticed_trial` with the same wording and scale, plus the similar constructs `rhelp`, `rused_trial`, and `elaborationhealth`), but they are asked about different label designs. This overlap is documented through the `in-grummon2023` and `grummon2023-similar` tags in [question_tags.json](../grummon2025/question_tags.json) and is not filtered from either dataset.

## Step 3. Question catalog, audit CSV, and manual verification in the Audit UI

To apply the same human-in-the-loop quality control used for Twin-2K-500 and Mega-Study, a script will generate `question_audit.csv`, `.audit_details.json`, `question_overrides.json`, and `question_tags.json` inside `code/data/nutrition/grummon2023/` so all $32$ candidate items can be inspected in `code/data/audit-app/`.

| Item group | Variables (`v`) | Count | Proposed `kev_type` | Preprocessing and audit notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vending machine selection | `bev_choice`, `snack_choice` | 2 | `choice` | $16$ branded options per task; arm-specific digital poster prepended to stem for `G`, `TLL`, `PA`, and `W`; exact product badges attached as `option_descriptions` |
| Single-product healthfulness | `bev_healthyR`, `bev_healthyY`, `bev_healthyG`, `snack_healthyR`, `snack_healthyY`, `snack_healthyG` | 6 | `score` | 5-point scale (`Very unhealthy` to `Very healthy`); redacted `.docx` brands resolved from codebook CSV (`Gatorade Fruit Punch`, `Gatorade G2 Grape`, `Schweppes Black Cherry Seltzer`, `Lay's Classic Potato Chips`, `Ritz Bits Cheese Cracker Sandwiches`, and `Snyder's Old Tyme Pretzels`) with arm-specific product badge in stem |
| Retrospective task noticing and use | `noticed_cal`, `noticed_trial`, `infl_bev`, `help_bev`, `infl_snack`, `help_snack` | 6 | Exclude (or review) | Tagged `#prior-task-dep` and `#has-dont-know` because stems ask what the participant noticed or used in the prior vending task and include `Not sure` (`2`) or `Did not see the label` (`2`); recommended `include = False` for standalone single-turn training |
| Standalone label perceptions, emotions, reactance, and stigma | `attn`, `elab`, `pme_disc`, `pme_enc`, `affect_worried`, `affect_scared`, `affect_guilty`, `affect_ashamed`, `affect_sad`, `learn`, `trust`, `control`, `react_manip`, `react_annoy`, `stigma_personal`, `stigma_obesity`, `stigma_blame`, `disgust` | 18 | `score` (or review `control`) | Self-contained once the arm's label stimulus description is prepended; uses singular (`this label`) in `Cal`, `G`, and `PA` and plural (`these labels`) in `TLL` and `W` per [VLOSurveyItems.docx](./ResearchBox%201895/Materials/VLOSurveyItems.docx); 3-point `control` tagged `#kev-type-check` |

Excluding the $6$ retrospective task-reference items leaves $26$ standalone items per respondent ($\times 5$ arms $= 130$ arm-item `qid`s and $206,493$ non-missing responses across $7,945$ respondents).

## Step 4. Deterministic encoding of visual FOPL stimuli, posters, and product badges

Every visual element shown to participants in Figure 1 of the main article and eFigure and eTable 1 of Supplement 2 is deterministically encoded into text from `BeverageCharacteristics.dta` and `SnackCharacteristics.dta` and documented in a companion `DATA.md` file.

| Trial arm (`FOPLname`) | Digital poster before selection tasks (`eFigure`) | Per-product front-of-package badge rule (`Figure 1` and `eTable 1`) | Arm-level label stimulus for standalone perception items |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Cal` | None | White Facts Up Front badge on every item reading `'<cal> CALORIES'` | White icon reading `'140 CALORIES PER SERVING'` |
| `G` | `Healthy Beverage/Snack Guide: Look for beverages/snacks with green labels for a healthier choice` showing green smiley `'CHOOSE OFTEN'` badge | `'<cal> CALORIES'` on all items, plus green badge with smiling face reading `'CHOOSE OFTEN'` when `item_col_str_jo == 'G'` (no second badge on `Y` or `R` items) | Green icon with smiling face reading `'CHOOSE OFTEN'` |
| `TLL` | `Healthy Beverage/Snack Guide: Look for beverages/snacks with green labels for a healthier choice` showing green `'CHOOSE OFTEN'`, yellow `'CHOOSE SOMETIMES'`, and red `'CHOOSE RARELY'` badges | `'<cal> CALORIES'` on all items, plus green smiling badge `'CHOOSE OFTEN'` (`G`), yellow neutral-face badge `'CHOOSE SOMETIMES'` (`Y`), or red frowning badge `'CHOOSE RARELY'` (`R`) | Three traffic-light icons: green smiling face `'CHOOSE OFTEN'`, yellow neutral face `'CHOOSE SOMETIMES'`, and red frowning face `'CHOOSE RARELY'` |
| `PA` | Orange brisk-walking guide (`'DID YOU KNOW IT TAKES 115 MINS OF BRISK WALKING TO WORK OFF A BOTTLE OF SODA? Look for labels with 0 minutes / fewer than 45 minutes for healthier choices'`) | `'<cal> CALORIES'` plus orange walking-person badge reading `'<item_phys_jo> MINUTES'` on every item | Orange icon with walking person reading `'63 MINUTES'` (minutes of brisk walking to burn calories) |
| `W` | `Healthy Beverage/Snack Guide: Look for beverages/snacks with no / fewer or no octagon labels for a healthier choice` showing black `'HIGH IN ...'` octagons | `'<cal> CALORIES'` on all items, plus black octagon warning badge(s) reading `'HIGH IN CALORIES'`, `'HIGH IN SUGAR'`, `'HIGH IN SATURATED FAT'`, and/or `'HIGH IN SODIUM'` for each `warn_* == 1` flag (no octagon when all four flags are `0`) | Black octagon warning icons reading `'HIGH IN CALORIES'`, `'HIGH IN SATURATED FAT'`, `'HIGH IN SUGAR'`, and `'HIGH IN SODIUM'` |

For `bev_choice` and `snack_choice` (`type = "choice"`), the option keys are the $16$ clean product names (`label_bev` and `label_snack`, with minor spacing typos such as `'Mountain Dew  - 20oz'` normalized) and `option_descriptions` stores each product's exact arm-specific badge string, mirroring `PRODUCT_BASE_LABELS` in Grummon et al. (2025). For `bev_healthy*` and `snack_healthy*` (`type = "score"`), the stem names the displayed product and its exact badge under the respondent's assigned arm. For the $18$ standalone label-perception items, the arm's label description is prepended to the stem and singular versus plural grammar (`this label` / `does` / `is` / `annoys` / `stigmatizes` / `increases` in `Cal`, `G`, and `PA` versus `these labels` / `do` / `are` / `annoy` / `stigmatize` / `increase` in `TLL` and `W`) is generated directly from the rule in [VLOSurveyItems.docx](./ResearchBox%201895/Materials/VLOSurveyItems.docx). Every `qid` is namespaced by arm as `f"grummon2023:{FOPLname}:{v}"` so empirical marginals never pool responses across distinct randomized label arms.

## Step 5. Respondent persona harmonization in `code/data/persona.py`

Respondent background variables in `grummon2023` map cleanly onto eight canonical global attributes in [persona.py](../../persona.py) plus two nutrition-specific attributes in `EXTRA_PERSONA_NAMES` of [base.py](../../base.py) that are shared with the Grummon et al. (2025) test set.

| Persona column | State label | Raw `grummon2023` source | Canonical mapped values | Non-missing ($N = 7,945$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `c_alphan` | `Country` | US eligibility | `'United States'` | $7,945$ |
| `SEX` | `Sex` | `gender` (`1..4`) | `'Female'` (`1`), `'Male'` (`2`), `None` (`3`, `4`, matching Grummon 2025) | $7,857$ (`1`/`2`) |
| `AGE` | `Age` | `age` ($\ge 18$) | `AGE_BINS = [17, 29, 49, 64, 120]` $\rightarrow$ `'18-29'`, `'30-49'`, `'50-64'`, `'65+'` | $7,945$ |
| `EDULEVEL` | `Education` | `educ` (`1..6`) | `'Less than high school'` (`1`), `'High school or some college'` (`2`, `3`), `'Associate\'s degree'` (`4`), `'College graduate/some postgrad'` (`5`, `6`) | $7,945$ |
| `RACE` | `Race` | `Race_1..Race_6` | Single-race `'White'` (`Race_5`), `'Black'` (`Race_3`), `'Asian'` (`Race_2` or `Race_4`, per `01_DataPrep.do`), and `'Other'` (`Race_1`, `Race_6`, or multiracial) | $7,944$ |
| `US_INC` | `Income` | `income_10cat` (`1..10`) | `'Less than $25,000'` (`1..3`), `'$25,000 - $34,999'` (`4`), `'$35,000 - $49,999'` (`5`), `'$50,000 - $75,000'` (`6`), `'$75,000 - $100,000'` (`7`), `'$100,000 or more'` (`8..10`), identical to Grummon 2025 | $7,934$ |
| `US_PRTY` | `Political party` | `partyid` (`1..8`) | `'Democrat'` (`1..3`), `'Independent'` (`4`), `'Republican'` (`5..7`), `'Other'` (`8`, mapped to `None` or `'Something else'` per `PERSONA_MAPS`) | $7,943$ |
| `HHSIZE` | `How many people, including yourself, live in your household?` | `hhsize_num` (`1..20`) | `'1'`, `'2'`, `'3'`, `'4'`, `'More than 4'` ($\ge 5$, matching Twin-2K-500 and WVS) | $7,944$ |
| `HISPANIC` | `Hispanic or Latino` | `latino` (`0`, `1`) | `'No'` (`0`), `'Yes'` (`1`), identical to Grummon 2025 | $7,944$ |
| `NFPUSE` | `Nutrition Facts label usage` | `NFPuse` (`1..5`) | `'Never'`, `'Rarely'`, `'Sometimes'`, `'Most of the time'`, `'Always'`, identical to Grummon 2025 | $7,944$ |

Crucially, `healthlit` in `grummon2023` is a 5-point self-reported medical reading-help item (`Never` to `Always`), whereas `HEALTHLIT` in Grummon et al. (2025) is an objective `0 out of 4` to `4 out of 4` Newest Vital Sign nutrition-label score; keeping `HEALTHLIT` unset (`NaN`) in `grummon2023` prevents conflating two incompatible scales under the same state key. Registering `grummon2023` in `DATASET_PERSONA_COLS` and `PERSONA_MAPS` inside [persona.py](../../persona.py) enforces strict value-set validation on load.

## Step 6. Shared training-pipeline updates in `base.py` and `marginals.py`

Inspecting [base.py](../../base.py) and [marginals.py](../../marginals.py) reveals two places where `option_descriptions` and `EXTRA_PERSONA_NAMES` (`HISPANIC` and `NFPUSE`) are currently supported in the evaluation builder (`build_long_records`) but omitted in the training builders.

First, in [base.py](../../base.py), `make_record` must copy `option_descriptions` from `row` (when present and non-null) so `make_view` passes per-product front-of-package badges into Kev `criteria` during training, `_record_chars` must include `option_descriptions` character lengths so `group_train_records` accurately bounds padded branch memory on 16-option vending tasks, and `build_persona_dataset` must iterate over `ALL_PERSONA_NAMES` rather than only `PERSONA_NAMES` so `HISPANIC` and `NFPUSE` are preserved in training records. Second, in [marginals.py](../../marginals.py), `build_marginal_lookup` must index all columns from `ALL_PERSONA_NAMES` present in `train_df` (missing attributes on ISSP, Twin-2K-500, and WVS rows remain `NaN` and are already skipped via `pd.notna(val)`). Regression tests must verify that existing ISSP, Twin-2K-500, and WVS records and marginal lookups remain byte-for-byte identical when no `EXTRA_PERSONA_NAMES` columns are populated.

## Step 7. Long-format Parquet export, loader module, and training integration

Finally, a reproducible preprocessing notebook `Grummon2023.ipynb` in `code/data/nutrition/grummon2023/` will read `question_audit.csv` and `question_overrides.json`, construct the long-format table, validate every `(state, question, criteria, target)` view through `KevEncoder.prepare(..., strict=True)` to confirm zero token truncation and exact option-target alignment, and write `grummon2023_US_long.parquet` with the standard columns (`CASEID`, `pid`, `arm`, the ten persona columns, `v`, `qid`, `label`, `question`, `type`, `option_codes`, `option_texts`, `option_descriptions`, `answer_code`, and `answer_text`).

A dedicated loader module `code/data/grummon2023.py` (mirroring [twin2k.py](../../twin2k.py)) will expose `load_grummon2023_df()`, `load_grummon2023_records()`, and `load_grummon2023_eval()`, setting `year = "grummon2023"` and `qid = "grummon2023:" + arm + ":" + v` (or preserving pre-built `qid`s) and calling `add_one_hot(df)`. Training notebooks can then concatenate `grummon2023_df` directly into `all_df` alongside `issp_df`, `twin_df`, and `wvs_df`, holding out a small family-stratified validation subset of `grummon2023` items while keeping [grummon2025_US_long.parquet](../grummon2025/grummon2025_US_long.parquet) untouched for out-of-study evaluation.
