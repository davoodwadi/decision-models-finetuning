# Grummon et al. (2025) codebook and stimulus metadata

This document records the exact variable mappings, front-of-package (FOP) nutrition label assignment rules, product nutritional profiles, and survey item wordings extracted from `ParentFile_wide.csv`, `ParentFile_wide.dta`, `DataDictionary.docx`, the IRB protocol (`zoi251032supp1_prod_1760453471.94614.pdf`, Supplement 1), and the Qualtrics instrument and supplemental tables (`zoi251032supp2_prod_1760453471.95614.pdf`, Supplement 2).

## Front-of-package labeling conditions and FDA Daily Value thresholds

Each participant in `ParentFile_wide.csv` is assigned to one of six trial arms recorded in `foplcond` (`DataDictionary.docx`, p. 1, where numeric codes 1 through 6 correspond to `HighIn`, `NutritionInfo`, `Positive`, `Positive+HighIn`, `Positive+NutritionInfo`, and `Spectrum`). Figure 1 of the main article (p. 4), Section 3.3.6.2 and Figure 3 of Supplement 1 (p. 1), and `eTable 1` of Supplement 2 (p. 3) specify the visual design and nutrient thresholds for each labeling system.

All nutrient-specific thresholds are defined relative to US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Daily Values (`%DV`) per serving, namely $20\text{ g}$ for saturated fat, $2,300\text{ mg}$ for sodium, and $50\text{ g}$ for added sugars. Under the `NutritionInfo` system, every product displays a white box with a magnifying glass icon and `FDA.gov` attribution that rates saturated fat, sodium, and added sugars per serving as `Low` with a green circle when the nutrient is below $5\%\text{ DV}$ ($< 1\text{ g}$ saturated fat, $< 115\text{ mg}$ sodium, or $< 2.5\text{ g}$ added sugars), `Med` with a yellow circle when the nutrient is between $5\%$ and $< 20\%\text{ DV}$ ($1\text{ to }3.9\text{ g}$ saturated fat, $115\text{ to }459\text{ mg}$ sodium, or $2.5\text{ to }9.9\text{ g}$ added sugars), and `High` with a red circle when the nutrient is at or above $20\%\text{ DV}$ ($\ge 4\text{ g}$ saturated fat, $\ge 460\text{ mg}$ sodium, or $\ge 10\text{ g}$ added sugars). Under the `HighIn` system, a white box with a magnifying glass icon reading `High In` and listing the specific nutrients exceeding $20\%\text{ DV}$ is displayed only when at least one of the three nutrients meets the $\ge 20\%\text{ DV}$ threshold, while products below $20\%\text{ DV}$ on all three nutrients carry no front-of-package badge. Under the `Positive` system, products earning 1, 2, or 3 stars under the commercial Guiding Stars algorithm display a blue badge reading `[* GOOD]`, `[** BETTER]`, or `[*** BEST]`, while products earning 0 Guiding Stars carry no badge. Under the `Spectrum` system, every product displays a color-coded 1-to-5 star badge derived from an expanded Guiding Stars algorithm that splits 0-star products into `[* POOR]` (1 star, red) and `[** FAIR]` (2 stars, orange) and maps 1-, 2-, and 3-star products to `[*** GOOD]` (3 stars, yellow), `[**** BETTER]` (4 stars, light green), and `[***** BEST]` (5 stars, dark green). The two combined arms (`Positive+NutritionInfo` and `Positive+HighIn`) display the blue `Positive` star badge (when earned) alongside the corresponding `NutritionInfo` or `HighIn` box.

For the 15 post-shopping perception, emotion, notice/use, policy support, and store realism items, the Qualtrics instrument (`Supplement 2, eTable 3`, pp. 7 to 9) displays example images of the labels from the respondent's assigned trial arm above the question text (`[display labels from participants randomly assigned trial arm]`). In `Grummon.ipynb` and `grummon_US_long.parquet`, this arm-level visual stimulus (`FOPL_STIMULUS[arm]`) is prepended to the question `instructions` as `Labels shown on products: <stimulus>`.

## Product-pair nutritional profiles and badges (`idbev` to `idmeal`)

In the six consumer understanding tasks (`Supplement 2, eTable 3`, pp. 6 to 7, and `DataDictionary.docx`, pp. 3 to 4), respondents compare two products from the same category and answer `Click on the product you think is healthier, or click 'They're the same' if you think the two products are equally healthy.` Each product image displays the exact badge assigned to that product under the participant's randomized trial arm. In `Grummon.ipynb` and `grummon_US_long.parquet`, these product-specific badges (`PRODUCT_BASE_LABELS`) are attached as option descriptions in `criteria` (with `"They're the same"` mapped to `None`). Option 1 is always the healthier product (`scr_id* = 1` when the respondent selects Option 1 and `0` otherwise).

## Respondent persona attributes (`state`)

Demographic and nutrition-related background variables are documented in `DataDictionary.docx` (pp. 2 to 3), `ParentFile_wide.dta` value labels, and `Supplement 2, eTable 3` (pp. 9 to 11). Five demographic attributes (`SEX`, `AGE`, `EDULEVEL`, `US_INC`, and `RACE`) overlap with the US-ISSP and Twin-2K-500 training datasets, while five attributes (`HISPANIC`, `SNAP`, `NFPUSE`, `ONLINEAMT`, and `HEALTHLIT`) are out-of-sample covariates specific to this nutrition trial. When exporting `grummon_US_long.parquet`, the shared attributes are mapped into the canonical global levels defined in `code/data/persona.py`.
