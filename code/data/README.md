# Data

Each source dataset sits in its own folder under `data/`. Raw survey downloads from GESIS sit in `data/<source>/raw/`, while manually verified metadata and processed outputs sit directly in each dataset folder. The `data_check_<year>.ipynb` notebooks in `code/` read the raw Stata files alongside the manually curated `question_bank.csv` and `label_fixes.csv` files to produce `questions_verified.csv` and `issp<year>_US_long.parquet`.

## Manually curated files

Each ISSP folder contains a manually verified `question_bank.csv` (and the exported `questions_verified.csv`) defining question wording, question types (`score`, `choice`, `noul`, or `exclude`), and scale direction (`reversed`). For waves where the Stata `.dta` file truncates value labels due to character limits (e.g., 2015, 2017, 2020, and 2021), `label_fixes.csv` provides manually verified label completions from the GESIS codebooks. Country names, nationality terms, and parliament names used to fill question placeholders are stored in `issp_countries.csv`.

## Train, validation, and test split (`code/splits/split.json`)

Across the 10 manually verified US waves (`issp2015_US_long.parquet` through `issp2024_US_long.parquet`, totalling 14,356 US respondents and 767,484 respondent-question observations), all 616 answered US questions are partitioned in `code/splits/split.json` into 494 `train` questions (80.2%), 50 `valid` questions (8.1%), and 72 `test` questions (11.7%). Standalone questions, two-item blocks, and cross-year recurring questions are assigned strictly to `train`. For multi-item batteries ($\ge 3$ questions sharing a stem, question type, and answer scale), one non-repeating item is held out for `test` and, for batteries of $\ge 4$ items, one additional non-repeating item is held out for `valid`, leaving at least two sibling items per battery in `train`.

## Global persona mapping (`code/data/persona.py`)

All training and evaluation datasets map respondent demographic attributes into a single canonical set of global persona levels defined in `code/data/persona.py`. Rather than overwriting raw or per-dataset files on disk, training and evaluation scripts call `map_persona_series(series, attr, dataset)` to validate and recode each dataset's stored values into the global levels at load time.

**Region harmonisation.** Raw ISSP records the nine US Census Bureau divisions, whereas Twin-2K-500 (`QID11`) groups California, Oregon, and Washington into `West` while isolating Hawaii and Alaska as `Pacific (HI, AK)`. Merging `Pacific (HI, AK)` into `West` in Twin-2K-500 and collapsing `East North Central` with `West North Central` into `Midwest` and `Mountain` with `Pacific` into `West` in ISSP yields an exact state-for-state partition into the four official US Census regions (`Northeast`, `Midwest`, `South`, `West`) across all datasets.

**Education harmonisation.** US surveys follow two different conventions, namely educational attainment (distinguishing `High school graduate` from `Some college, no degree`, as in Twin-2K-500, Covid, and Grummon) versus highest completed credential (`US_DEGR` and `EDULEVEL` in the NORC General Social Survey and ISSP, where anyone whose highest completed degree is a high school diploma or GED is coded as `Upper secondary`, regardless of whether they attended some college without earning a degree). Splitting ISSP `Upper secondary` respondents via raw years of schooling (`EDUCYRS`) is not clean because 182 `Upper secondary` respondents in the 2015 to 2019 waves report 9 to 11 years of schooling (GED recipients) and 165 respondents across all waves report 16 to 20 years of schooling without a college degree. Defining four global credential-based levels (`Less than high school`, `High school or some college`, `Associate's degree`, and `College graduate/some postgrad`) aligns all five datasets without ambiguity while preserving SubPOP's evaluation labels verbatim.

**Race and ancestry separation.** ISSP's `US_ETHN1` (`Ancestry`) records 96 national or regional ancestral origins (`Germany`, `England and Wales`, `Ireland`, `Mexico`, etc.) rather than US Census racial categories, whereas Twin-2K-500 (`QID15`), SubPOP (`RACE`), Covid, and Grummon record US racial/ethnic groups (`White`, `Black`, `Asian`, `Hispanic`, `Other`). Because national ancestry and racial classification are distinct constructs, `US_ETHN1` (`Ancestry`) and `RACE` (`Race`) are maintained as separate persona attributes.
