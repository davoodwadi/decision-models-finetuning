# Plan for decoupled logical-persona and micro-chunked marginal training (`train-v2-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m`)

This document outlines the statistical motivation, optimization mechanics, cross-scale parity guarantees, and additive implementation plan for [train-v2-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m.ipynb](../train-notebooks/train-v2-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m.ipynb). Building on the grouped training analysis in [train-grouped-diversevalid-800m.md](./train-grouped-diversevalid-800m.md) and the empirical $k$-marginal framework in [train-diversevalid-marginals-800m.md](./train-diversevalid-marginals-800m.md), the new regime decouples logical persona group construction from GPU micro-batch execution while leaving all existing trainers and notebooks untouched.

## Why coupled group construction conflates training regime with GPU memory

In the existing pipeline, `group_train_records` in [data/base.py](../data/base.py) and `GroupedTrainer.train_one_step` in [training/trainer.py](../training/trainer.py) treat a single GPU shared-prefix forward pass bounded by `(max_questions_per_group, max_chars_per_group)` as the logical persona update unit. On the combined `ISSP + Twin-2K + Twin mega + Grummon 2023` corpus (`2,283,849` training records across `4,472` questions and `24,151` unique full personas when `include_country = False`), coupling logical persona groups to a single forward pass creates three distinct bottlenecks.

**Topic clustering from global length sorting.** Because `group_train_records` sorts all answered questions of a respondent by exact formatted character count `_record_chars(r)` prior to slicing, multi-item survey batteries with nearly identical prompt lengths (e.g., Twin-2K `QID9` cereal items, `QID25` personality items, or repeated ISSP scale batteries) are un-shuffled back into narrow single-battery chunks. In the baseline `v1` configuration (`max_questions_per_group = 32`, `max_chars_per_group = 20_000`), groups average only `23.94` questions and `15.85` distinct question stems because `2,800`-character Grummon 2023 vending prompts and long Twin mega stimuli prematurely close groups under the `20,000`-character ceiling.

**Unnecessary activation recomputation on smaller backbones.** Raising `max_questions_per_group` and `max_chars_per_group` directly inside a single `model.forward(..., shared_prefix = True)` call expands the padded branch tensor `[Q, L_b]`. Above roughly `28,000` padded characters on `jaredpalmer/kev-0.8b`, avoiding out-of-memory failures forces `gradient_checkpointing = True` in [kev/kev/shared_prefix.py](../kev/kev/shared_prefix.py), replaying every hybrid Gated DeltaNet and full-attention layer during backward and cutting training throughput nearly in half (from roughly `150` questions per second to `79` questions per second) despite `0.8B` weights occupying less than `2` GiB.

**Confounded optimization trajectories across model scales.** Most importantly for scaling from `0.8B` to `4B` and `9B`, coupling logical group size to VRAM capacity previously forced each backbone onto a different optimization regime, e.g., `max_questions_per_group = 32` on `0.8B` (`7,949` steps per epoch, `287.3` questions per step), `max_questions_per_group = 16` with `max_chars_per_group = 8_000` on `4B` (`>12,000` steps per epoch, `~155` questions per step), and `max_questions_per_group = 96` with `gradient_checkpointing = True` on `9B` (`~3,500` steps per epoch, `~535` questions per step). Changing `questions_per_step` and `total_steps` across model sizes alters learning-rate schedules, subpersona draw counts, and AdamW second-moment damping $\sqrt{\hat{v}_t}$ on `PointerHead`, confounding backbone capacity with batch structure.

## Decoupling logical persona groups from VRAM micro-chunks

Separating the statistical grouping unit from the GPU execution unit eliminates all three bottlenecks without altering the mathematical objective. At the logical regime level, each unique persona $i$ with $N_i$ answered questions has its records randomly shuffled via the epoch RNG and partitioned into

$$K_i = \left\lceil \frac{N_i}{Q_{\text{logical}}} \right\rceil$$

balanced contiguous logical groups of size $\lfloor N_i / K_i \rfloor$ or $\lceil N_i / K_i \rceil$ (e.g., a respondent with `90` questions under $Q_{\text{logical}} = 80$ splits into `45 + 45` rather than `80 + 10`), with no `max_chars` cap at the logical level. Because $K_i$ depends solely on $N_i$ and `questions_per_group`, both `groups_per_epoch` and `steps_per_epoch` are deterministic constants across random seeds, epochs, and model sizes. Under marginal supervision, each logical group $g_p$ draws a single granularity `k_choice` from `k_schedule = (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, "full", "full")`, samples one `subpersona` of $k$ observed traits, and receives exact empirical choice probabilities $\bar{p}_S(q)$ via `augment_group_with_marginals` in [data/marginals.py](../data/marginals.py). Each optimizer step draws $P = \text{personas\_per\_step} = 12$ logical groups $g_1, \dots, g_P$ totaling $N_{\text{step}} = \sum_{p=1}^{P} |g_p|$ questions.

At the hardware execution level inside `train_one_step`, each logical group $g_p$ first samples a single persona attribute permutation from `step_rng` to construct one shared `state` string for all questions in $g_p$. The questions inside $g_p$ are then sorted ascending by `_record_chars(r)` and greedily packed into $C_p$ VRAM-safe micro-chunks $c_{p, 1}, \dots, c_{p, C_p}$ satisfying $|c_{p, j}| \le \text{micro\_max\_questions}$ and $|c_{p, j}| \cdot \max_{r \in c_{p, j}} \text{chars}(r) \le \text{micro\_max\_chars}$ (with any single ultra-long question exceeding `micro_max_chars` placed in a size-1 micro-chunk). Because the step cross-entropy objective decomposes additively across micro-chunks,

$$\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{step}} = \nabla_\theta \left( \frac{1}{N_{\text{step}}} \sum_{p=1}^{P} \sum_{q \in g_p} \ell(z_{p, q}, t_{p, q}) \right) = \sum_{p=1}^{P} \sum_{j=1}^{C_p} \nabla_\theta \left( \frac{1}{N_{\text{step}}} \sum_{q \in c_{p, j}} \ell(z_{p, q}, t_{p, q}) \right),$$

calling `.backward()` on each micro-chunk's normalized loss sum and immediately freeing intermediate activations accumulates the exact same gradient as a single unchunked forward-backward pass over all $|g_p|$ questions (identical to floating-point precision when `lora_dropout = 0`, and identical in expectation under `lora_dropout = 0.05`), while bounding peak GPU memory strictly by `(micro_max_questions, micro_max_chars)`. Sorting by length only within each shuffled logical group preserves full cross-battery topic diversity across the optimizer step while restoring tight branch padding inside each GPU tensor.

## Non-destructive software architecture

**Preserving existing modules via surgical hunk removal.** No existing training logic in [training/trainer.py](../training/trainer.py), [training/marginal_trainer.py](../training/marginal_trainer.py), [training/iid_trainer.py](../training/iid_trainer.py), [data/base.py](../data/base.py), or [data/marginals.py](../data/marginals.py) will be overwritten, and file-level `git checkout` or `git restore` must not be used because the working tree holds uncommitted `v1` and audit changes while [train-v1-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m.ipynb](../train-notebooks/train-v1-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m.ipynb) is untracked. Instead, targeted line-level edits will remove strictly the `SUPPORTED_GROUP_FN_NAMES`, `validate_group_fn`, `group_fn`, and `_base_epoch_groups_for` hunks from `base.py`, `marginals.py`, `trainer.py`, and `marginal_trainer.py` (plus the corresponding `group_fn` description in [train-diversevalid-marginals-800m.md](./train-diversevalid-marginals-800m.md)), while preserving every uncommitted `v1` and audit hunk intact, namely the Grummon 2023 `EXTRA_PERSONA_NAMES` entries (`CHILDREN`, `WIC`, `DIETQUAL`, `VENDING_BEV`, `VENDING_SNACK`, and `BMI`), `option_descs_or_none` for `NaN` handling after concatenating datasets, the updated `build_persona_dataset` cache key (`tuple(df.columns)` and `ALL_PERSONA_NAMES`), `normalize_eval_sets` and `val_name is None` handling when zero evaluation sets specify `valid = True`, and the symmetric missing-key check in `_verify_resume_signature`.

**Standalone `code/training/chunked_trainer.py` module.** Following the subclass pattern of [training/iid_trainer.py](../training/iid_trainer.py), all new functionality will live in `code/training/chunked_trainer.py` and be re-exported from [training/__init__.py](../training/__init__.py). The module will provide `group_persona_logical`, `build_logical_marginal_groups`, `split_logical_group_into_microchunks`, `make_state_chunk_view` (which combines a pre-sampled logical `state` string with one micro-chunk's question specs without re-shuffling persona traits across chunks), `ChunkedGroupedTrainer(GroupedTrainer)`, and `ChunkedMarginalTrainer(ChunkedGroupedTrainer)`.

**Strict fail-fast validation and resume invariants.** Every constructor and helper will validate positive integer bounds (`questions_per_group >= 1`, `personas_per_step >= 1`, `micro_max_questions >= 1`, `micro_max_chars >= 1`, `micro_batch_size >= 1`), non-empty `personas` dictionaries, exact persona equality inside each logical group, and `PersonaMarginalLookup` type checks with zero silent fallbacks. In `_run_signature`, logical regime parameters (`trainer` set to `"chunked_grouped"` or `"chunked_marginal"`, `questions_per_group`, `personas_per_step`, `total_steps`, `seed`, `lr`, `epochs`, and `k_schedule`) are strictly enforced on `resume = True`, whereas pure VRAM execution parameters (`micro_max_questions`, `micro_max_chars`, `micro_batch_size`, `gradient_checkpointing`) are recorded in `describe()` and checkpoint `metrics.json` `settings` and can be adjusted across GPUs or tuned via `probe_vram(micro_grid)` and `set_microbatching(...)` without altering `total_steps` or invalidating mid-epoch checkpoint resumption. Crucially, no draw from `epoch_rng` or `step_rng` (`self.rng`) may depend on how a logical group is chunked, meaning persona attribute order is drawn strictly once per logical group and never once per micro-chunk. Under `lora_dropout = 0.05`, PyTorch and CUDA random states still advance once per micro-chunk forward pass, so a resumed run with unchanged micro-settings is bit-identical, whereas resuming with modified micro-settings preserves exact logical batches, subpersonas, and attribute orders and remains statistically valid without being bit-identical.

## Planned v2 800M notebook setup and verification protocol

**Target `v2` configuration.** Once approved for implementation, [train-v2-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m.ipynb](../train-notebooks/train-v2-issp-twin-mega-grummon-train-marginals-800m.ipynb) will instantiate `ChunkedMarginalTrainer` saving to `runs/kev-0.8b-v2-issp+twin2k+mega+grummon2023-marginals` on the identical `2,283,849` training records and `[valid_grouped, grummon2025_grouped]` evaluation sets as `v1`, configured with `base_model = "jaredpalmer/kev-0.8b"`, `lora_rank = 64`, `weights_dtype = "bf16"`, `use_bf16 = True`, `gradient_checkpointing = False`, `epochs = 1` (`3,311` steps), `lr = 5e-5` under `OneCycleLR` (`pct_start = 0.1`), `questions_per_group = 80`, `personas_per_step = 12`, `micro_max_questions = 32`, `micro_max_chars = 20_000`, `micro_batch_size = 1`, `k_schedule = DEFAULT_K_SCHEDULE`, `val_every = [150, 300]` (evaluating `valid` ~22 times and `grummon2025` ~11 times across `3,311` steps, matching `v1`'s validation resolution), and `save_every = 50`. When scaling to `4B` and `9B`, `questions_per_group = 80`, `personas_per_step = 12`, `seed = 0`, and `total_steps = 3,311` remain unchanged, adjusting only `(micro_max_questions = 16, micro_max_chars = 8_000, gradient_checkpointing = False)` on `4B` and `(micro_max_questions = 48, micro_max_chars = 24_000, gradient_checkpointing = True)` on `9B`.

**Pre-run verification checks.** Prior to launching the full `0.8B` run, three automated checks will verify correctness: first, an fp32 gradient equivalence test (`lora_dropout = 0`) confirming that a single unchunked `80`-question group and its multi-chunk `(16, 4_000)` decomposition produce identical parameter gradients to floating-point precision (`max |diff| < 1e-5`); second, a deterministic `resume = True` test verifying mid-epoch state recovery and fail-fast signature mismatch rejection; and third, a CUDA `probe_vram` sweep confirming steady-state peak VRAM and step throughput on RTX 5090.
